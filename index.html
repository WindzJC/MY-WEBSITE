THE READER JOURNEY

Discovery creates interest. Give it a destination.

Amazon, social media, interviews and book trailers can all introduce the work. Your author website connects those moments in one place the reader can understand and return to.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    A recommendation, interview, retailer page or trailer puts the book in front of someone new.

  2. 02

    Interest

    The cover, premise or atmosphere gives that reader a reason to look for more.

  3. 03

    A clear destination

    One author home brings the books, story, reading order and trusted buying links together.

A website does not create an audience by itself.

It gives the interest you already earn somewhere useful to go.

OPTIONAL EXAMPLESee what a three-point review looks like+

This example uses a made-up novel, The Lantern Keeper. Your review is written about your actual book.

  1. Lead with your hook. Move the most intriguing idea—the lighthouse keeper receiving letters from her future self—to the first line of the book description.
  2. Give readers somewhere to go. Connect the author story, book information and next release instead of sending every visitor straight to one retailer page.
  3. Prepare one useful launch asset. Turn the strongest visual premise into a concise teaser rather than creating generic posts for every channel.

FAQ

Questions authors ask.

Is the review really free?

Yes. We review your book, website, retailer pages and purchase path, then send the three improvements we’d make first. Where it helps, we may include a small visual concept. There’s no obligation to go further.

Do I need to know whether I need a website or a trailer?

No. Tell us about the book and we’ll recommend the website, trailer, cover design or combination that best addresses the priorities.

How long does a project take?

Timelines depend on the book, approved creative scope and content readiness. We’ll confirm the schedule and revisions for your specific project in writing.

Do you film live-action trailers?

No. Astra trailers are editing-led, built from licensed or author-approved footage, music and sound design. That keeps them cinematic and affordable.

What happens after the review?

If you want us to build the recommendation, we send a fixed quote with the deliverables, price, timeline and revisions. Paid work begins only after you approve it.

Do you guarantee book sales?

No one honestly can. We guarantee the agreed creative work, revisions and delivery. Sales and traffic depend on many things beyond a website or trailer.

Will a website sell my books?

Not on its own. But when someone hears about your book through a trailer, an interview or a recommendation, your website gives them somewhere to go. They can discover your other books, get to know you and find where to buy. It’s the destination your other marketing can point toward.
