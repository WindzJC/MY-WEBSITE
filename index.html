FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Five concise answers before you request a concept.

What do you need from me?
An official book link, digital copy, blurb, author summary, and any important images or links you want considered.
Is the initial concept free?
Yes. It is a no-obligation strategic concept direction showing how the author and book could be presented. It does not include a completed website or trailer for free; paid full development begins only after the scope and cost are agreed.
Can the website feature more than one book?
Yes. The author website can bring current books, purchase links, media, and future releases together under one professional author platform. See what an author website can include.
What happens after I approve the direction?
We confirm the content, deliverables, timeline, payment structure, revision rounds, domain, and launch plan before development begins.
Do you guarantee book sales or traffic?
Astra guarantees the agreed creative work, functionality, revisions, and delivery. Sales, traffic, and audience response depend on the book, audience, platforms, promotion, and other external factors.
