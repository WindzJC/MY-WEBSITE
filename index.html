What This Fixes

Most books do not need more promotion first. They need clearer positioning.

We start with the hook, promise, and audience, then build visuals that carry the same message across every touchpoint. The goal is simple: make the book easier to understand, easier to trust, and easier to buy.

What these assets help fix

  • An unclear book promise
  • A launch that looks scattered across platforms
  • A weak visual first impression
  • Too many steps from interest to purchase
  • Promo materials that do not feel aligned

What the work is meant to improve

  • Faster understanding of the book’s appeal
  • Stronger trust through premium presentation
  • Cleaner conversion paths with clearer CTAs
  • More consistent branding across launch materials
  • A more professional long-term author presence

Results vary by genre, traffic source, audience quality, and platform.
