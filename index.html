The Astra Method

THE ASTRA METHOD

The Astra Strategic Author Audit

Diagnose the gap → expose the consequence → prescribe the fix → define the result → demonstrate the direction

  1. 01

    Research

    We review the book, author, website, retailer presence, reviews, recognition, media, current projects, and purchase path using public information and anything the author supplies.

  2. 02

    Diagnose

    We identify where attention, credibility, reader understanding, or the next step is being lost.

  3. 03

    Prioritize

    We identify the three improvements we would address first and explain why.

  4. 04

    Demonstrate

    Where useful, Astra creates a focused no-charge concept so the author can see the recommendation in practice. The concept is a strategic demonstration, not a complete free website, trailer, or production project.

  5. 05

    Build

    Only if the direction makes sense do we define the paid scope, timeline, deliverables, and implementation.

No paid development begins until scope, deliverables, price, and timeline are agreed.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does Astra review?
Astra reviews the book, author, current website, retailer presence, reviews, recognition, media, current projects, and purchase path using public information and anything the author supplies.
Is the initial Strategic Author Audit free?
Yes. The initial review identifies the strongest priorities and strategic direction. A focused concept may be provided where it helps demonstrate the recommendation; it is not automatically a completed website, finished trailer, or full free production project.
Do I need to know whether I need a website, trailer, or something else?
No. Astra starts with the project and reader journey, then recommends the website, redesign, book experience, trailer, launch direction, or combination that best addresses the priorities.
What happens if I want Astra to build the recommendation?
Astra defines the scope, deliverables, price, timeline, and revision plan with you. Paid implementation begins only after those terms are agreed.
Do you guarantee book sales or traffic?
Astra guarantees the agreed creative work, functionality, revisions, and delivery. Sales, traffic, and audience response depend on factors outside the website or trailer itself.
