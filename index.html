FAQ

Questions authors ask.

Is the review really free?

Yes. We review your book, website, retailer pages and purchase path, then send the three improvements we’d make first. Where it helps, we may include a small visual concept. There’s no obligation to go further.

Do I need to know whether I need a website or a trailer?

No. Tell us about the book and we’ll recommend the website, trailer, launch graphics or combination that will make the biggest difference.

How long does a project take?

Author websites typically take 3–5 days and trailers 5–7 days once the content and scope are approved. Website + Trailer packages typically take 7–10 days.

Do you film live-action trailers?

No. Astra trailers are editing-led, built from licensed or author-approved footage, music and sound design. That keeps them cinematic and affordable.

What happens after the review?

If you want us to build the recommendation, we send a fixed quote with the deliverables, price, timeline and revisions. Paid work begins only after you approve it.

Do you guarantee book sales?

No one honestly can. We guarantee the agreed creative work, revisions and delivery. Sales and traffic depend on many things beyond a website or trailer.
