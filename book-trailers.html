BOOK TRAILERS / STORY-FIRST EDITING

Make readers feel the story before they open the book.

A cinematic book trailer should create curiosity, not retell every chapter. Astra builds short, intentional edits shaped around a verified premise, genre, visual direction, and the audience you want to reach.

START WITH THE STORY

The right mood. The right moment. The right cut.

Scripted hooks, intentional editing, typography, licensed or client-approved visuals, and sound design create a memorable introduction to the book—while avoiding spoilers and unverified story details.

Astra’s standard service is an editing-led book teaser or trailer, not a live-action shoot with actors or an original film crew. Special elements, including narration, bespoke illustration, or custom footage, must be discussed and quoted separately.

A SIMPLE, APPROVAL-LED PROCESS

From manuscript to master edit.

01

Read and research

Review the blurb, provided story material, audience, and verified book details to avoid invented characters or scenes.

02

Approve the script

Agree on the hook, outline, proposed narration where applicable, and the creative direction before the edit begins.

03

Build and refine

Edit approved visuals, sound, timing, and screen text. Review the draft and refine the agreed deliverables.

04

Deliver for launch

Supply the contracted widescreen and vertical formats, with a practical plan for using them on the author website and social channels.
