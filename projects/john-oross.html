PROJECT STORY

What Astra set out to create

The challenge was to give a 636-page multidisciplinary collection, selected writing, a biography, and purchase options one clear public destination.

Astra built an editorial author platform with a hierarchy that connects John Oross, Wandering Thoughts, and his broader writing. The site guides visitors from discovery to available purchasing choices without inventing promotional outcomes.
