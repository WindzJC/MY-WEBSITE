01 / THE PROJECT

Many forms of writing.
A coherent introduction.

Wandering Thoughts brings together poetry, prose, lyrics, stories and reflection. The author website gives the collection, John’s identity and his wider writing a shared public home.

The design connects book presentation, biography, writing sections and reader links. The result is an editorial platform with more than one way into the work.

02 / INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE

From a name to a next step.

Three clear routes in: the book, the writing and the author.

  1. 01 / ARRIVE

    Meet John.

    A prominent author name and book title establish whose work this is. The introduction gives readers a concise sense of the collection.

    Visit site → About John ↗
  2. 02 / EXPLORE

    Choose a thread.

    The Book, Explore, About John and Journal organize different reasons to visit, from discovering the collection to learning about the writer.

    Visit site → Explore / Journal ↗
  3. 03 / CONTINUE

    Find the book.

    Explore the Book and Get Your Copy offer distinct next steps. A separate Get the Book navigation action keeps the purchase path easy to find.

    Visit site → The Book ↗

03 / VISUAL DIRECTION

Space for the words.
Presence for the book.

The homepage uses warm paper tones, dark serif typography and generous spacing. The book cover supplies the strongest color and a tangible focal point beside the author’s name.

The hierarchy is deliberate and readable: identity first, context second, action third.
