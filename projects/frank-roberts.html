CLIENT PROJECT / AUTHOR WEBSITE + BOOK TEASER

Frank Roberts — The Girls with Black Hair Saga

A Marine Corps veteran’s four-book romantic saga, introduced with a cinematic teaser and given one home online.

Client
Frank Roberts
Series
Girls with Black Hair (four books)
Astra delivered
Author website and cinematic book teaser
Website includes
Featured book, series overview, embedded teaser, author biography, event contact form and newsletter sign-up

THE BRIEF

One place for the whole saga.

Book one, The Girl with the Black Hair, was published, with three more books planned. Frank needed one place where readers could meet him, understand the four-book saga, watch the teaser and buy the first book.

WHAT ASTRA CREATED

A teaser and a website that work together.

We cut a cinematic teaser that sets the mood of the first book without giving the story away. Then we built Frank’s author website around it: the featured book with Amazon and Barnes & Noble links, a series page that maps all four books, the teaser, his story from small-town Massachusetts to the Marine Corps and decades of international work, and sign-ups so readers hear when the next books arrive.

THE RESULT

A complete author platform.

Frank now has a finished website and teaser he can share anywhere he promotes the saga, with a clear path from first impression to buying book one.
