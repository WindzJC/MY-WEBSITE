01 / THE PROJECT

A doorway into
a conspiracy novel.

The Queen of Intelligence: A 9/11 Conspiracy Novel is the book at the center of this completed Astra trailer. The published piece gives Harvey Havel’s story a short visual introduction.

The creative emphasis is tension and curiosity: an entry point into the premise, with room left for the reader to discover the novel itself.

02 / CREATIVE DIRECTION

Intrigue before explanation.

A focused hook, deliberate pacing and a clear connection back to the book.

01 — THE HOOK

Open a question.

A focused introduction keeps the premise approachable. The viewer needs a reason to continue before they need the entire story.

02 — THE ATMOSPHERE

Give tension a rhythm.

Visual storytelling, music, sound design and deliberate pacing form the project’s documented creative scope.

03 — THE BOOK

Keep the title in view.

On-screen typography connects the cinematic presentation to the novel. The trailer is an invitation to explore the book further.
