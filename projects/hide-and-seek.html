01 / THE VISUAL WORLD

Small in the frame.
Surrounded by possibility.

In the published trailer still, tall trees tower over a figure seen from below. Muted greens, deep shadows and the surrounding branches make the environment feel close and unsettled.

Those are visible qualities of the image, not a synopsis. The title and published trailer are the focus of this project study; the image leaves the story open.

02 / A CLOSER LOOK

The setting does
the talking.

COMPOSITION

Look upward.

The low viewpoint lets the trees dominate the frame. The surrounding space becomes as noticeable as the person within it.

PALETTE

Stay in the shadows.

The still’s restrained green tones and dark edges give it a quiet, uneasy atmosphere without explaining what happens next.

EDITORIAL LENS

Watch for the reveal.

View the full trailer to see how shot order, duration and sound change the meaning of an individual image. A still captures atmosphere; the edit supplies time.
