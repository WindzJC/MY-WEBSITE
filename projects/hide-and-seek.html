FRAMES FROM THE PUBLISHED TRAILER

Movement. Isolation. Suspense.

The completed video moves between a figure in motion, the forest canopy, and the mystery of a closed door.

Swipe to view the film frames on mobile.

01 / THE VISUAL WORLD

Small in the frame.
Surrounded by possibility.

In the published trailer still, tall trees tower over a figure seen from below. Muted greens, deep shadows and the surrounding branches make the environment feel close and unsettled.

The figure anchors the foreground while trunks and branches draw the eye into the canopy. The forest becomes a commanding presence.

02 / INSIDE THE FRAME

Scale. Shadow. Suspense.

01 / VIEWPOINT

Below the canopy.

The low angle puts the viewer beneath the trees. Their height gives the frame its imposing scale.

02 / COLOR

A restrained palette.

Muted greens and deep shadows hold the image together; the warmer jacket gives the eye a human focal point.
