# Astra Author Spotlight — proposed pilot operations

Status: proposal, NOT a blanket inclusion in current contracts. Do not advertise as a live guaranteed service until capacity, permissions and channel rights are confirmed.

## Complimentary pilot: one coordinated organic round
- Eligible finished and approved projects: Astra website feature with book/author context, consented media, and checked destination links.
- Facebook: one organic announcement if the author approves the copy and assets.
- YouTube: one completed trailer upload on Astra's channel only if an approved finished trailer exists and the rights allow distribution; offer author-first publishing when preferred.
- TikTok: one short vertical post only if the existing contracted deliverables contain a suitable approved vertical cut. Do not add uncompensated video editing.
- Website-only projects: no promised trailer creation or TikTok video.
- An author can decline any platform. Agree on publication timing in writing.
- No guaranteed sales, impressions, reach or platform engagement. No included ad spend or monthly posting.
- Existing signed client deliverables are not automatically amended.

## Reusable fulfillment checklist
1. Author opts in and approves name, bio, book text, covers, project attribution, external URLs, and publication timing.
2. Check rights for every channel: images, artwork, footage, music, performances, voice-over, and any publisher restrictions.
3. Decide upload sequence (author-first or Astra-first); confirm permissions for YouTube and short-form redistribution.
4. Produce a single publishing kit from already-agreed deliverables: title, short caption, accurate genre, author credit, verified links, platform-native sizes.
5. Author approves preview of each piece. Save approval evidence and final agreed timing.
6. Publish only the selected channels; log URLs and dates in the client project record.
7. Optional light follow-up: report directly observable link-click and organic engagement signals, without claiming attribution for untracked sales.
8. Archive approvals and delivery notes; do not promise recurring posts.

## Paid scope under consideration (NOT YET ON SALE)
Astra Launch Boost: optional one-time content cutdowns, captions, graphics, scheduling and basic reporting. Pilot with one consenting author, estimate labor and platform workload, then price sustainably. Explicitly separate any advertising spend and permissions.

Recurring campaign management: future exploration only; do not promise paid ad setup, ongoing organic distribution, engagement moderation or month-to-month reporting before staffing and procedures are validated.

## Website release checklist
- Confirm social channels and publication rights.
- Confirm this pilot language and limits with Astra management.
- Update preview content and inspect desktop/mobile layouts.
- Obtain business approval prior to merging with production.
