THE QUESTION AUTHORS ASK FIRST

“My books are already on Amazon.
Why a website?”

Keep your Amazon listings. A retailer page is built to sell one book. Your website introduces the author behind every book, then sends readers to Amazon or wherever they prefer to buy.

A retailer pageYour author website

  1. 01The focus

    A retailer page: Each page is built around one book.

    Your author website: Your whole catalogue in one place: every book, every series.

  2. 02The bigger picture

    A retailer page: Series order, your story and your other books sit on separate pages.

    Your author website: Readers find the series order, your other books, your story and your trailers or interviews together.

  3. 03The link you share

    A retailer page: A different link for every book and every store.

    Your author website: One address to share in interviews, on social media and in every promotion.

  4. 04Your next book

    A retailer page: Each new release starts on a new page.

    Your author website: Each new release joins the same home, so the site grows with your catalogue.

A website won’t bring readers to you on its own. It gives every reader who is already curious a clear place to go next.
