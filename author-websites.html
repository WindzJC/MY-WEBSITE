FAQ

Author website questions

Can the website feature multiple books?
Yes. One book, a series, or a growing catalogue can be organized under the same author platform.
What do you need from me?
An official book link or digital copy, biography, approved images, purchase links, and any verified media or reviews you want considered.
Is the concept free?
Where a visual demonstration would help explain the recommendation, Astra may create a focused no-charge concept. It is a strategic demonstration, not a completed website. Paid development begins only after the scope and cost are agreed.
What happens after I approve the direction?
The content, deliverables, final price, timeline, payment structure, revision rounds, domain, and launch plan are confirmed before paid development begins.
Can you use my existing domain?
Yes, when access and the current domain setup allow it. Domain connection details are confirmed during scoping.
Who owns the final website?
Ownership, website files, account access, and any third-party licenses are confirmed in the agreed project scope before work begins.
Do you guarantee sales or traffic?
No. Astra guarantees the agreed creative work, functionality, revisions, and delivery. Sales and traffic depend on the book, audience, promotion, platforms, and other external factors.
