THE CREATIVE STANDARD

Different genres. One standard of care.

We build around each book's audience and story, not a fixed visual template. Film footage, editing, narration, graphics, and production methods are described according to what was actually supplied.

Portfolio entries show the creative work, not guaranteed book sales or campaign results. The gallery links to publicly available Astra Productions work. Authors, rights, and book information will be reviewed before any expanded promotional use.
